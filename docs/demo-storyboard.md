## Cursor choreography

1. Before recording, start both backend and frontend; wait until the interface shows **CALIBRATED MODEL API**. Reset to T+08, 2×, no completed controls, **Replay twin** selected.
2. Move the cursor on straight, deliberate paths. Each travel should take 350–550 ms; pause 250 ms before a click.
3. In the incident shot, select **1×**, click **Run incident**, and use Space to pause on the first predictive-alert frame at T+09. Capture that clean plate. Then select **4×**, advance to T+18, pause, and capture a second clean plate. A cut between the two states is intentional and truthful.
4. Use only these implemented interactions in the hero cut: reset, speed, run/pause, Explain this alert, navigation keys 1/3/4/5, Open response studio, select Hold hot-work permit, Record approved dry run, the three simulated role approvals, and Export evidence pack.
5. Do not click Search, Open graph inspector, plant selector, profile, or standard-reference links; they are disabled or outside the demonstrated flow. Validation metric toggles are functional but unnecessary in the locked cut.
6. For each view, record a five-second cursor-free handle before and after the action. This gives the editor room for reframing and captions.

## Visual language

- Use the product's graphite, safe-green, amber, and critical-red palette. Editorial callouts should reuse those colors and never cover an app label.
- Captions: Inter SemiBold, 54–64 px at 1080p for thesis cards; 34–40 px for metric callouts; 28–32 px for the disclosure strip. Keep all text inside a 10% title-safe margin.
- Use one persistent disclosure strip, lower left, on a 72% charcoal capsule. It must remain readable on every product shot.
- Prefer slow 108–120% punch-ins and match cuts. Avoid glitch effects, fake CCTV noise, sirens, explosion stock footage, or injured-worker imagery.
- If a callout is not a literal UI value, prefix it with `REPLAY`, `SIMULATED BENCHMARK`, or `DESIGN`.

## Sound direction

- Original or properly licensed minimal industrial-electronic bed, approximately 92 BPM, no vocals. Keep it at roughly −28 LUFS under narration and automate lower under dense metric lines.
- Narration target: −16 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP maximum, gentle compression around 3:1, high-pass near 70 Hz, and conservative de-essing.
- Use only three UI accents: opening evidence pulse, predictive-notification chime, and human-approval confirmation. No alarm sound—the point is that the legacy alarm is still clear.
- Leave 250–400 ms of near-silence before “Every device says safe” and before the final sentence.
